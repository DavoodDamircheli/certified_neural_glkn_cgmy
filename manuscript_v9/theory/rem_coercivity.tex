\begin{remark}[Explicit coercivity constant]\label{rem:coercivity}
Lemma~\ref{lem:LK} also sharpens Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-coercivity}.  Using $1-\cos u\ge2u^2/\pi^2$
on $|u|\le\pi$,
\[
 I_a(\xi)\ \ge\ \frac{2}{\pi^2}\gamma_Y\,a^{Y-2}\xi^2\ (|\xi|\le a),\qquad
 I_a(\xi)\ \ge\ \frac{2e^{-\pi}}{(2-Y)\pi^Y}\,|\xi|^Y\ (|\xi|\ge a),\qquad
 \gamma_Y:=\int_0^\pi u^{1-Y}e^{-u}du,
\]
which yield the explicit constant $c_1=5.1\times10^{-5}$ of \eqref{eq:c1-explicit} on the box of
Table~\ref{tab:proposed-box} with a half-page proof (Appendix~\ref{app:proof-uniform}), against
$1.6\times10^{-8}$ for the earlier high-frequency argument; the optimal constant, attained at
$\xi=0$, is $r_{\min}/(1+M_{\max}^{Y_{\max}}+G_{\max}^{Y_{\max}})=1.40\times10^{-4}$.
\end{remark}
