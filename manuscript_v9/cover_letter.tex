\begin{letter}{Editorial Board\\SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, \emph{Symbol-based residual certification of reduced and
neural surrogates for tempered-fractional CGMY pricing}, for publication in SIAM Journal on
Scientific Computing.

The paper certifies reduced and neural approximations of European-put price trajectories under the
CGMY model by residual bounds for the spline Galerkin discretisation of the tempered-fractional
pricing equation. Its contribution relative to the certified reduced-basis literature is that the
discretisation is shift-invariant, so that the whole certification apparatus is read off one
explicit periodised Fourier symbol: the Galerkin matrices are assembled exactly as Toeplitz sections
of that symbol (with a stated truncation bound), the coercivity constant of the symmetric part is a
closed-form Lévy--Khintchine lower bound that replaces the successive constraint method, a banded
comparison norm built from the mass and stiffness symbols gives an $O(N)$ certificate with explicit
effectivity, and empirical interpolation acts on the symbol, a function of one variable, so that the
non-affine dependence on the tempering and activity parameters $(G,M,Y)$ becomes affine in about 36
terms with a rigorous error transfer to the operator norm. The resulting reduced model is certified
at a cost independent of the resolution, and a three-stage rule escalates to the full-order bounds
only when needed.

The numerical part is a matched evaluation at the resolution that prices the parameter box to one
cent: the full-order certificate costs 6\% of a direct solve, a certified POD--Galerkin model of rank
80 runs at 3\% of it and settles 99.8\% of $10^4$ queries without touching the full space, the
certificate detects out-of-distribution queries without any assumption on where a query lies, and
the neural reduced models trained for the same task fail the tolerance by a factor of at least 46,
a negative result that the certificate establishes query by query and explains. No false accept
occurs on any of the 414 queries with a reference solution. A residual certificate for the American
put in the same framework is proved as an outlook.

Every number in the paper is read from a result file by a checked script, and a public archive
regenerates every table and figure from those files and reruns the verification study in about half
an hour on one core; the archive address is given in the reproducibility section.

An earlier version of this work, with a numerically estimated coercivity constant and a
principal-period assembly, was [circulated as a preprint / not previously submitted]; the present
manuscript replaces its spectral estimate and positivity audit by theorems, its assembly by the exact
one, and its benchmark by the matched production study, and is not under consideration elsewhere.
Suggested referees can be provided on request.
