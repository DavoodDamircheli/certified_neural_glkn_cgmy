Title of paper: Symbol-based residual certification of reduced and neural surrogates for tempered-fractional CGMY pricing
Authors: Davood Damircheli

File: supplement.pdf
Type: PDF document (8 pages)
Contents: Supporting material for the main article: the notation table; the full verification studies (assembly against a 40-digit reference, spectral-bound and comparison-norm checks with two figures, iteration counts of the Riesz and time-step solvers, initial-error tests and the case Y<1); a stress test certifying a DeepONet trained on a different discretisation; tables of all neural reduced-model checkpoints and their training details; operation counts and measured constants of the cost model; and three extensions not used in the main article (discrete parameter stability, other time schemes, preconditioned correction).
Reason for inclusion: lets readers inspect the full verification data and training details behind the summaries in Section 5 without lengthening the main article. Every result claimed in the main article is proved there; nothing in it depends on the proofs in the supplement.
